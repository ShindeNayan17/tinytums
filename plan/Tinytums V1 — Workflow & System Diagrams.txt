read-only / masked / section-specific / temporary
Audit event
who, when, context, resource, action, outcome
Key V1 rules
• Mother controls ongoing sharing with caregivers and external/connected professionals
• Active admission permits authorised institutional staff access via the episode
• Caregiver is read-only
• Discharge removes/reduces temporary team access
• Verified clinical fields cannot be silently overwritten by mother
